Call

Call tomorrow's chart

Five tracks enter the chart. Your job is to place them in the order you believe they'll finish, sealed before the day begins. The counts stay hidden until it settles, so nobody gets to wait for the answer and then discover they knew it all along. Call all five in exact order and you land at the top. Miss by a little and you can still place: all five in any order, or four, or three, or just the number one track. The pot pays in USDC, and on any day nobody makes the perfect call, the top allocation rolls forward and waits for somebody with better instincts. Or better luck disguised as instincts.

Spins · Embers · The Draw

The goods.

What a dollar gets you, the thing we're staying quiet about, the draw that pays out every night, and how the money reaches your wallet.

Spins

A Spin opens one paid session in the catalog. Fifty of them cost a dollar, which is cheaper than your last gas fee and considerably cheaper than a therapist who has never been rugged.

We cap the dopamine on purpose. Spending the whole day inside the clinic is its own diagnosis, and the staff are already overwhelmed. Spins are play credits with no cash value of their own.

Embers

Every paid session mints an Ember. They never reset and can't be bought, so they end up being proof you listened while APESONUS was still strange enough to be interesting.

What do they do? We'll tell you when we decide. The vault appears to know more.

The Draw

Every eligible Spin is also a ticket. Five names get pulled each night, weighted by how much you played, and the first name out takes the biggest allocation. Winners are paid in USDC. Pure luck this one, no chart reading required, and no technical analysis thread is going to save you.

The pick is seeded from the chain after the day closes, so anyone with a calculator and trust issues can re-run it and land on the same five names. We insist on being checked. It's a therapy thing.

Getting paid

Winnings sit in your balance in dollars until you want them. Past five dollars, drop in a Solana address and ask, and the USDC goes out from the house wallet, with the transaction on record so you can check it landed.

Check the address twice. Crypto remains deeply committed to making typos permanent.
